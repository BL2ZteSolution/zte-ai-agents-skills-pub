# Judgement Criteria

## Pass

Use when TSSR is complete and no major deployment readiness risk is found.

## Conditional Pass

Use when minor survey gap exists with manageable follow-up.

## Pending

Use when TSSR or key survey evidence is missing.

## Reject

Use when wrong site, major missing survey sections, or major deployment blocker exists.

## Manual Check Required

Use when survey risk cannot be safely judged.

## Common Rules

- Access or installation constraint may require Reject or Manual Check Required.
- Missing key survey evidence must be Pending.
- Do not claim engineering approval.
