# Evidence Rules

## Acceptable Evidence

- TSSR document or compact summary.
- Site survey report.
- Site access/constraint evidence summary.
- Survey photo evidence summary.

## Weak Evidence

- Survey evidence without site identity.
- Missing photos or constraint details.
- Ambiguous installation notes.

## Invalid Evidence

- Wrong site.
- Major missing survey sections.
- Survey evidence showing unresolved deployment blocker.

## Risk Flags

- `missing_tssr`
- `incomplete_survey`
- `access_constraint`
- `installation_constraint`
- `site_mismatch`
- `missing_photo_evidence`
- `manual_verification_required`
