# Evidence Rules

## Purpose

Define acceptable, weak, invalid, and missing evidence for Step 11: Housekeeping.

## Acceptable Evidence

- User confirms housekeeping completed.
- Photo/evidence summary confirms site cleanup.
- Admin/ZTE PIC confirms housekeeping done.
- Site left safe/clean status is stated.

## Weak Evidence

- "Done" without housekeeping context.
- No site context.
- Photo is unclear.
- Safety/cleanup status is missing.

## Invalid Evidence

- Unsafe site condition.
- Unclean site.
- Leftover material or waste.
- Wrong site.
- Housekeeping not done.

## Missing Evidence

- No housekeeping confirmation.
- No cleanup/restoration evidence.
- No safe leaving status.
- No site/session context.

## Risk Flags

- `missing_housekeeping_confirmation`
- `unclear_housekeeping_evidence`
- `unsafe_to_leave`
- `unclean_site`
- `leftover_material`
- `site_mismatch`
- `manual_verification_required`

## Evidence Decision Notes

Housekeeping is critical before check-out. Do not proceed if site safety, cleanup, or restoration condition is unclear.
