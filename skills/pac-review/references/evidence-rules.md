# Evidence Rules

## Acceptable Evidence

- PAC document or compact summary.
- Acceptance checklist.
- Site/link/scope information.
- Supporting completion evidence.
- Customer/PIC confirmation if available.

## Weak Evidence

- PAC document without scope context.
- Acceptance statement without evidence.
- Missing customer/PIC confirmation where required.

## Invalid Evidence

- Wrong site or scope.
- Invalid document/template.
- Major missing acceptance evidence.
- Contradictory completion evidence.

## Risk Flags

- `missing_pac_document`
- `missing_acceptance_evidence`
- `scope_mismatch`
- `site_mismatch`
- `incomplete_pac_fields`
- `customer_acceptance_risk`
- `manual_verification_required`
