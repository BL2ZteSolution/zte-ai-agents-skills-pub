# Judgement Criteria

## Pass

Use when PAC evidence is complete and consistent.

## Conditional Pass

Use when minor gaps exist but acceptance preparation can continue with clear conditions.

## Pending

Use when required PAC document or evidence is missing.

## Reject

Use when wrong site/scope, major missing acceptance evidence, invalid document, or major acceptance risk exists.

## Manual Check Required

Use when acceptance readiness cannot be safely judged.

## Common Rules

- Do not claim customer acceptance.
- Major scope mismatch must not pass.
- Missing mandatory PAC evidence must be Pending.
