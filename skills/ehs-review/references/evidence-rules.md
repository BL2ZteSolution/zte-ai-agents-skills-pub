# Evidence Rules

## Acceptable Evidence

- EHS report/checklist.
- EHS login screenshot or compact summary.
- Safety/PPE evidence photo summary.
- Site-specific safety confirmation.
- Admin/ZTE PIC confirmation.

## Weak Evidence

- Safety statement without site identity.
- PPE evidence unclear or cropped.
- EHS screenshot without visible status.

## Invalid Evidence

- Unsafe condition visible or reported.
- Wrong-site EHS evidence.
- Invalid or contradictory EHS evidence.
- Missing critical safety confirmation.

## Risk Flags

- `missing_ehs_report`
- `missing_safety_evidence`
- `unsafe_condition`
- `ppe_issue`
- `site_mismatch`
- `unclear_evidence`
- `manual_verification_required`
