# Judgement Criteria

## Pass

Use when EHS evidence is complete and no safety blocker exists.

## Conditional Pass

Use when minor evidence gap exists with no immediate safety blocker.

## Pending

Use when EHS report or required safety evidence is missing.

## Reject

Use when unsafe condition, wrong site, invalid EHS evidence, or critical safety non-compliance exists.

## Manual Check Required

Use when safety status cannot be safely judged by AI.

## Common Rules

- Critical safety uncertainty must not pass.
- Unsafe condition must be Reject or Manual Check Required.
- Missing mandatory safety evidence must be Pending.
