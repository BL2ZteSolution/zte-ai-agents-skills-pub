# Evidence Rules

## Acceptable Evidence

- Pathloss report/file summary.
- Link identity and Site A/Site B context.
- Frequency/band/path planning summary when available.
- Supporting microwave planning evidence.

## Weak Evidence

- Pathloss evidence without link identity.
- Missing Site A or Site B.
- Planning context too sparse to judge.

## Invalid Evidence

- Wrong link/site.
- Major missing planning evidence.
- Major technical readiness risk.

## Risk Flags

- `missing_pathloss_report`
- `link_mismatch`
- `site_a_site_b_mismatch`
- `incomplete_path_profile`
- `missing_frequency_or_band_context`
- `planning_risk`
- `manual_verification_required`
