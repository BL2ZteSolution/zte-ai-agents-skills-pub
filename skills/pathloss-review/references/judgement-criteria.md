# Judgement Criteria

## Pass

Use when Pathloss evidence is complete enough and link/site context is consistent.

## Conditional Pass

Use when minor planning/evidence gap exists with clear follow-up.

## Pending

Use when required Pathloss evidence or link context is missing.

## Reject

Use when wrong link/site, major missing planning evidence, or major technical readiness risk exists.

## Manual Check Required

Use when technical planning risk cannot be safely judged by AI.

## Common Rules

- Do not claim engineering approval.
- Do not calculate final RF design.
- Major link mismatch must not pass.
